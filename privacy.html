Privacy Policy Moderator-IG · Last updated: September 21, 2026 Moderator-IG is an Instagram comment moderation application used to assist with moderating comments on authorized Instagram Professional accounts. Information We May Process When an Instagram Professional account authorizes Moderator-IG, the application may process information made available through the Instagram API, including: Instagram account identifiers and usernames Instagram media or post identifiers Comment identifiers and comment text Commenter usernames or identifiers made available by the API Access tokens required to communicate with the Instagram API Moderation decisions and technical logs needed to operate the service How We Use Information Information is used only to provide, test, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Instagram comment moderation functionality for authorized accounts. If AI-assisted moderation is enabled, comment text may be transmitted to the configured AI service provider for classification or moderation analysis. Moderator-IG does not sell Instagram user data. Data Retention Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, test, troubleshoot, or secure the service, or as required by law. Data that is no longer needed may be deleted or anonymized. Data Security Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect stored credentials and other application data. Access tokens and application secrets should not be publicly disclosed. Data Deletion To request deletion of information associated with an Instagram account, follow the instructions on the Data Deletion Instructions page. Changes to This Policy This policy may be updated as Moderator-IG changes. The "Last updated" date above will be revised when material changes are made.
